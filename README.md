## 🔬 Featured Research Projects

### 🧠 [Generative Models & Discrete Diffusion for Text](https://github.com/omarsafi3/Generative_models_and_diffusion_for_text)
> **Python • PyTorch • HuggingFace Transformers • DiffusionBERT • LLaDA • LLM Benchmarking**
* Implemented and trained transformer-based bidirectional discrete text diffusion models (~125M parameters).
* Built a block-sequential decoder inspired by Multi-Block Diffusion, reducing denoising steps from 512 to 60 for a **1.54x inference speedup** (398 vs. 614 ms/sample).
* Applied GPT-2 negative log-likelihood as an inference-time energy signal to guide candidate selection, achieving **40.17% ROUGE-L** and **10.83% BLEU-4** across 200 aligned Question-Title benchmark evaluations.
* [🔗 View Repository](https://github.com/omarsafi3/Generative_models_and_diffusion_for_text)

### 🫁 [Federated Deep Learning for Chest X-Ray Classification](https://github.com/omarsafi3/Federated_Chest_XRay_Classification)
> **Python • TensorFlow 2.15 • Flower • SE-ResNet50V2 • Non-IID Dirichlet Partitioning**
* Designed a 4-client federated learning pipeline for 4-class chest X-ray diagnosis with channel attention (SE) and multi-scale feature fusion.
* Achieved **90.4% mean test accuracy** and **89.5% Macro-F1** across 3 seeds in centralized benchmarks on 1,705 held-out test images.
* Formulated an inverse validation-loss aggregation strategy that achieved **90.0% accuracy** and **88.5% Macro-F1** under controlled non-IID label skew ($\alpha=0.5$), improving Macro-F1 by **+0.6 percentage points** over standard FedAvg and approaching centralized accuracy within **0.4 percentage points**.
* [🔗 View Repository](https://github.com/omarsafi3/Federated_Chest_XRay_Classification)
